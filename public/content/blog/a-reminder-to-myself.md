Periodically, I feel emotionally restless, overwhelmed and depressed. I am not writing this reminder to reject these feelings, which I used to consider as bad and unhealthy, comparing with the sense of delight and achievement when I ultimately come up with something original, creative and critical. I shall realise these feelings are entangled in such a way that one can not come into being without the other, since each comes up with its own materiality and is situated in my life reality.

As a reminder to myself, I hope not to forget the sense of delight and excitement in those specific moments that I get well with myself, that I feel truly alive, thoughtful, and deeply engaged. Try to be easy and chill with those grey, try not to reject or feel guilty about them, think about the tangible stuff that can be truly touched and felt, whether it's a physical traffic card that can take you far, a small conversation that brings people together or a simple personal presentation learned in Swedish.
